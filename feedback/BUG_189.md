#### Resolution Notes

1. Replaced missing unicode power and ground glyphs with standard text labels "3V3" and "GND".
2. Positioned "1", "3V3", and "GND" next to their respective physical pins on the J14 header cutout, derived dynamically from `enclosure_gpio_pin_pitch`.
3. Rotated "1", "3V3", and "GND" 90 degrees (rotation 0.0) relative to the vertical GPIO header orientation so they read horizontally pointing into the pins.
4. Repositioned the "GPIO" header title to the outer left margin to eliminate overlap with pin labels.
5. Parameterized all GPIO label offsets, font sizes, pin pitches, and engraving depths in `measurements.yaml` and `CarrierBoardConfig`.
6. Added regression test `test_regression_bug_189_enclosure_top_gpio_labels` in `src/projects/tests/test_carrier_board.py`.
