#### Description

Sheet 2 shows a Q1 transistor going to a VLOAD_SW on the NINA. VLOAD_SW has no pin marker, and there is no VLOAD_SW in the NINA docs. I think this is an artifact of when the PCIe express connector was populated. Can we remove it and correct the BLE module footprint and pinout? We can use modem commands to power on or off the NINA; no need for a separate GPIO

#### Resolution Notes

Removed legacy VLOAD_SW net, pruned VLOAD_SW from U11 pin breakouts and Sheet 2, connected U11 VCC directly to 3V3 system rail, corrected NINA pin 18 from VLOAD_SW to SWITCH_2 in ic.yaml, and pruned VLOAD_SW traces and vias from routing.yaml.
