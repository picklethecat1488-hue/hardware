#### Description

We have some extra space on the back side of the board. Can we harden the power delivery system of the board a bit with some decoupling caps, ESD diodes on hot-pluggable connectors, etc? We should think about not just brownout and ESD protection of the carrier board itself, but the potential for brownouts with external sidecars connected with cameras, IMUs, speakers, etc.

#### Resolution Notes

Added decoupling caps C15, C16, C17 and ESD TVS protection diode D8 to carrier board bottom side
