#### Resolution Notes

- **Obstacle Avoidance**: Constructed `obstacle_boxes` in `src/provider/schematic_diagram.py` combining component bounding boxes, title block (`202.0, 12.0, 83.0, 32.0`), decoupling card, and truth tables. Vertical GND and power trunks dynamically query obstacles and stop short with safe margins instead of cutting through tables or title blocks.
- **Discrete Component Sizing**: Standardized discrete symbol dimensions in `schematic_diagram.py`: horizontal resistor zig-zag width reduced from 20.0mm to 8.0mm (`r_half_w = 4.0`), amplitude to +/-1.6mm; capacitor plate height reduced from 15.0mm to 7.0mm (`plate_h = 3.5`), matching C6 in the decoupling capacitor bank; transistor Q1 gate and channel bars scaled down to 7.0mm and 9.0mm.
- **Pullup Stage Antennas**: Terminated pull-up vertical wire segments at `y_top_rail = y_zz_top + 6.0` in `src/provider/schematic/passives.py`, matching the horizontal crossbar and eliminating top rail overshoot stubs.
- **Sheet 17 Sub-Sheet Split**: Replaced dense single sheet with 4 clean sub-sheets in `pcb.yaml`:
  - Sheet 17: Status and Overrides - MCU Reset & Bootloader (SW1, JP1, JP2)
  - Sheet 18: Status and Overrides - Power Isolation & Boot Mode (JP3, JP4)
  - Sheet 19: Status and Overrides - Primary Power LEDs (D2, D3, D4, R7, R8, R9)
  - Sheet 20: Status and Overrides - Peripheral & MCU LEDs (D5, D6, D7, R10, R11, R12)
- **Pin Side Configuration**: Configured `pin_sides: {'1': left, '2': left}` for JP1-JP4 and D2-D7, eliminating right-side pin collisions between adjacent components.
- **Schematic DRC Text Overlap Check**: Added `compute_text_bounding_boxes` to `SchematicBoundingBoxCalculator` and `SchematicDiagram`, and integrated `SCHEMATIC_TEXT_COLLISION` and `SCHEMATIC_PAGE_BOUNDARY_EXCEEDED` checks in `PCBDesignRulesChecker.check_schematic`.
- **Wire Routing in Same-Column**: Updated `SchematicWireSegmentPlanner` in `src/provider/schematic/wire_router.py` to route collinear vertical connections directly without redundant loopbacks.
- **Regression Unit Test**: Added `test_regression_bug_206_off_sheet_schematic_feedback` in `src/tests/test_pcb_advanced.py`.
