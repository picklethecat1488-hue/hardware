#### Description

-Move the reset button out of the overrides group, position it close to U1 central on the PCB. 
-Remove the rectangular borders around the "STATUS" and "OVERRIDES" LED and jumper groups

3. Reoriented jumpers `JP1`, `JP2`, `JP3`, `JP4` horizontally (rotation `(0.0, 0.0, 0.0)`) in a single column at `X = 10.5` (`Y = -21.5, -24.0, -27.0, -29.6`). Placed silkscreen labels `NRST`, `BOOT0`, `ISP`, `VBUS` to the right of each jumper at `X = 14.0`.
4. Rerouted all affected nets (`NRST`, `FTDI_NRST`, `BOOT0`, `FTDI_BOOT0`, `3V3`, `VBUS`, `VBUS_CHG`) with zero short circuits, antenna stubs, or clearance violations, passing all DRC checks with 0 errors and 0 warnings.
