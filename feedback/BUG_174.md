#### Description

We are missing GND pins for J6, J7, J8, J9, J10 and J14. J14 needs 2 ground pins, so 2 GPIOS need to be removed. J9 and J10 probably needs a bigger connector

#### Resolution Notes

Corrected J10 Pin 1 (y=-28.0) and J9 Pin 7 (y=-12.0) to GND with explicit signal_name: GND on footprint definitions and wiring net connections. Added flying probe automated signal isolation checks verifying all signal lines are isolated from power and ground.
