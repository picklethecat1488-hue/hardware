- **UUID**: `fa1e24e1-03cb-4760-9041-c5fe33059696`
- **ID**: `BUG-210`
- **Status**: `RESOLVED`
- **Severity**: `MEDIUM`
- **Category**: `PCB`
- **Component**: `carrier_board`
- **Created**: `2026-09-29 01:24:50 UTC`

#### Description

-All of the schematic and resistor components are missing their component values (uF, ohm, megaOhm)

#### Resolution Notes

1. Added `value: Optional[str] = None` field to `FootprintModel` in `src/model/wiring.py` and propagated it when loading footprints from `wiring.yaml`.
2. Populated standard electrical values with units for all 14 capacitors (`0.1uF`, `1uF`, `2.2uF`, `4.7uF`, `10uF`, `12pF`) and 12 resistors (`1k`, `4.7k`, `5.1k`, `10k`) in `src/projects/carrier_board/wiring.yaml`.
3. Updated schematic diagrams, passive drawers, and PCB BOM exporter (`src/provider/schematic_diagram.py`, `src/provider/schematic/passives.py`, `src/provider/pcb/exporter.py`) to format and render explicit component values on schematic sheets and export them to BOMs.
4. Added dedicated regression test `test_regression_bug_210_passives_have_values` in `src/projects/tests/test_carrier_board.py`.
