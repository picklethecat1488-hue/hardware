# 🟢 `[BUG-209]` Schematic and PCB silkscreening unreadable despite DRC checks passing

- **UUID**: `128614eb-9f13-4cc4-b4f3-68558e04722c`
- **ID**: `BUG-209`
- **Status**: `RESOLVED`
- **Severity**: `MEDIUM`
- **Category**: `PCB`
- **Component**: `carrier_board`
- **Created**: `2026-09-29 01:17:12 UTC`

#### Description

-Circled in GREEN on Sheet 19, where schematic text overlaps other text.
-Circled in YELLOW on Sheet 19, where VBAT and LED_VBAT_K appear to be shorted together because the wire routing is missing a "T" junction that would move it further away from the diode.
-Circled in GREEN on PCB Board texture, the newly added jumper and LED regions are spaced too close to each other, with text overlapping each other as well as the existing "LAYER 1-6 RIGID FLEX" silkscreening
-CIrcled in ORANGE on PCB board texture, the newly added jumper and LED regions should be expanded to fill this area, to relieve the silkscreening issues

#### Reproduction Steps

1. Build schematic:
2. $ python src/build.py "carrier_board:pcb"
3. View board texture:
4. $ python src/view.py  carrier_board/carrier_board:view/simulate

#### Resolution Notes

1. Excluded LED cathode and anode nets (`LED_*`, `*_K`, `*_CATHODE`, `*_ANODE`) from `PowerNetMatcher` in `src/provider/schematic/constants.py`, eliminating duplicate power rail flags and text overlap on Sheet 19 and 20.
2. Increased left-left detour offset `x_col` in `src/provider/schematic/wire_router.py` to 8.0mm, creating a clean "T" junction and moving the cathode wire away from the diode body.
3. Relocated `LAYER 1-6 RIGID-FLEX` from `(0.0, -28.0)` to `(-8.0, -34.5)` in `src/projects/carrier_board/provider.py`, clearing the LED and jumper corridors.
4. Resized STATUS grouping frame to `(4.5, -25.0), (6.2, 13.5)` and OVERRIDES grouping frame to `(12.2, -23.0), (7.8, 17.5)`, providing a clear 0.70mm separation gap.
5. Repositioned STATUS and OVERRIDES group titles to `(4.5, -32.8)` and `(12.2, -32.8)` above top borders; formatted jumper and switch labels horizontally (`NRST`, `BOOT0`, `ISP`, `VBUS`, `RESET`) with >1.15mm pad clearance; moved refdes to bottom silkscreen to eliminate top silkscreen clutter.
6. Added dedicated regression test `test_regression_bug_209_silkscreen_readability_and_spacing` in `src/projects/tests/test_carrier_board.py`.
